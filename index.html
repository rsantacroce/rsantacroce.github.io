Roberto Santacroce Martins

Programmer. I contribute to open source in my spare time, mostly around Bitcoin and local-first software.

I'm a programmer from Brazil, living in Madrid. I've been writing software for a little over twenty years and I still enjoy it.

In my free time I contribute to open source, mainly Drivechain (BIP300/301) and Layer Two Labs sidechains, and I build small tools I want to use myself: local-first apps, a Lisp for personal data, and a few games.

I mostly write Rust, C/C++, Swift and TypeScript, and I like software that runs locally and doesn't need permission to exist.
